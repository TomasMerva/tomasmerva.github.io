<ProjectCard
  title="Globally-Guided Geometric Fabrics for Reactive Mobile Manipulation in Dynamic Environments"

<ProjectCard

<ProjectCard
  title="Reactive Grasp and Motion Planning for Adaptive Mobile Manipulation Among Obstacles"
  href="https://youtu.be/AldMFKnlW3M"
  imageSrc="/images/projects/rgf/multi_fabrics_grasp.png"
  imageAlt="Dinova"

  description="The Reactive Grasp and Motion Generation (RGF) framework enables real-time, 
  adaptive grasping and motion planning for mobile manipulators operating 
  in close proximity. By combining vision-based grasp analysis, nonlinear optimization,

/>
